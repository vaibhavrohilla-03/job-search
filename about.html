About JobSift

A deliberately small job search: one box, readable results, and a direct link to the employer.

What this is

JobSift is a personal, non-commercial project. It is a single-page interface for searching job listings that are published publicly by employers and job boards, with the goal of making them easier to read and compare on a phone or a slow connection. There is no account system, no resume database, and no paid placement.

Where listings come from

Listings are collected from public job feeds and partner search APIs that are intended for syndication and republication. Where a partner requires attribution or a link back to the source, this site does exactly that: every result points to the original posting page, and applications are always completed on the advertiser's own site.

  • Job search and listing data is provided by third-party feeds and partner APIs.
  • Excerpts are shortened; full descriptions are never republished.
  • Salary figures appear only when the source publishes them.
  • Listings that no longer resolve at the source are removed.

What this is not

JobSift does not charge candidates, does not resell candidate data, and does not act as a recruiter or staffing agency. It is not affiliated with the employers listed. If a listing looks wrong or outdated, please tell us and it will be corrected or removed.

Technical notes

The site is a static build — plain HTML, CSS and a small amount of JavaScript — served from GitHub Pages. It sets no advertising cookies and runs no third-party analytics. Any search performed here is stateless: nothing is stored on the server.
